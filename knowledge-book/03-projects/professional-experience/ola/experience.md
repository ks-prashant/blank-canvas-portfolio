# Ola — Product Analyst, Driver Billing Pod

**Identity:** Ola (ride-hailing). Role: Product Analyst. 04/2019–04/2021. Sat within the driver billing pod — the team responsible for how drivers are paid, billed, and reconciled.

**Status:** All items below shipped or delivered during his tenure.

---

## Overview

Ola is a ride-hailing company. Within it, the driver billing pod owned everything related to how a ride's fare is calculated, billed, and reconciled with the driver — a domain that touches pricing, compliance, and driver trust simultaneously. His work here spans four distinct pieces of that pod's mandate: a fare-prediction feature, a billing-compliance simulation effort, the pod's analytics infrastructure, and a specific ownership role inside a multi-country market launch. These were four separate initiatives, not one blended project — each is described on its own below.

**Who he worked with:** his core stakeholders were **engineering** and the **business finance team** — the latter being a direct consequence of owning billing logic, where the product decision and the financial reconciliation are the same problem seen from two sides. This engineering-plus-the-money-side stakeholder shape recurs across his later roles too; see `../../../05-methodology.md`, "How he works with people."

## 1. ML Fare Prediction

**Problem:** Ride fares were calculated with rule-based logic that was often inaccurate, leading directly to billing disputes and rider/driver complaints.

**Approach:** Ideated and shipped a **decision-tree model** trained on real driver-behavior data — signals like route and speed — to predict the ride fare upfront, replacing the older rule-based logic. Worked directly with engineers and business stakeholders, owning model design, integration into the billing workflow, and the payment-recovery logic downstream of billing. Rolled out across the **top 5 cities.**

**Outcome:**
- Cut billing disputes by **$200k/year.**
- Reduced complaints by **40%.**

## 2. Billing Workflow Simulation & Compliance

**Problem:** The driver billing workflow needed to be verified for accuracy and regulatory/policy compliance — errors here directly affect driver pay and trust.

**Approach:** Simulated the driver billing workflow end to end and coordinated the resulting fixes with engineering to bring it into compliance.

**Outcome:**
- Improved bill accuracy from **91% to ~99%.**

## 3. Analytics Dashboards for the Driver Billing Pod

**Problem:** Product decisions for the pod needed visibility into funnel health, retention, and feature performance — this was separate infrastructure work, not a byproduct of the fare-prediction or billing-compliance efforts.

**Approach:** Built **20+ funnel, retention, and feature dashboards** used to drive product decisions across the driver billing pod.

**Outcome:** Gave the pod ongoing visibility into performance rather than one-off analysis — the infrastructure behind repeated product decisions over his tenure, not a single feature launch.

## 4. EU Market Launch — Billing & Driver Onboarding Tracking

**Problem/context:** Ola launched and scaled cab services in three EU-market countries. This was a team effort, not something he owned end to end — but within it, billing and driver-onboarding tracking fell under his domain.

**His ownership:** Owned the steps related to **billing** and **driver-onboarding tracking** specifically, as part of the broader multi-country launch team.

**Outcome:** Part of the team that successfully launched and scaled cab services across three EU-market countries.

## What this demonstrates

Across all four pieces: the ability to lead ML-driven product features end to end (model design through production billing integration, bridging data science and product directly without a separate ML-PM layer); to own compliance-critical simulation and fix-coordination work; to build and maintain the analytics infrastructure a product pod runs on, as its own ongoing responsibility; and to hold a specific, scoped ownership role (billing, driver onboarding) inside a larger cross-functional launch rather than claiming the whole launch as his own.
