# Checkout & Payment-Mode Optimization — nurture.farm

**Identity:** Built at nurture.farm, an MSME marketplace app under UPL. Owned end to end by Prashant Singh, Product Manager (promoted from APM during this tenure). 05/2021–11/2022.

**Status:** Shipped, live across the retailer base during his tenure.

---

## 1. Problem

Retailers on the marketplace faced limited payment options, a complicated checkout flow, and high drop-off at the payment step.

## 2. Approach

Integrated new payment gateways and payment modes, then ran A/B tests across three specific points in the checkout flow:
- **Payment-mode ordering** — which payment options are shown, and in what order.
- **Amount-calculation UI** — how the final payable amount is presented to the retailer.
- **Address-page flow** — the delivery-address step of checkout.

## 3. Outcome

Reduced checkout friction and payment-step drop-off. This work fed the same north-star metric as the rest of the payments and monetization roadmap — see `company-context.md` for the roadmap-level MTU outcome (4k→8k) that this, cashback, and lending all contributed to together.

## 4. What this demonstrates

Funnel-level experimentation discipline — treating checkout as a sequence of individually testable steps rather than one monolithic flow, and running structured A/B tests at each step rather than redesigning the whole flow on intuition.

## 5. One-line pitch

Ran structured A/B tests across payment-mode ordering, amount-calculation UI, and the address-page flow for an MSME marketplace's checkout, reducing friction and drop-off at the payment step.
