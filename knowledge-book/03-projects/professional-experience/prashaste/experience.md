# Prashaste — Associate Consultant / Business Analyst

**Identity:** Consulting role at Prashaste. 11/2017–01/2019.

**Status:** Both items below delivered during his tenure, for multiple dealership clients.

---

## Overview

Prashaste is a consulting firm; his engagements were with **auto dealerships** of top auto-makers, covering two distinct pieces of work — a broad rollout of sales/finance tracking tools, and a smaller set of deep business-evaluation engagements. These were separate initiatives, not one blended project.

## 1. Sales & Finance Trackers, at Scale

**Approach:** Built sales and finance trackers for **200+ dealerships** of top auto-makers, and personally delivered **25+ training sessions** to dealership staff on how to use them.

**Outcome:** Trackers and training delivered across 200+ dealerships — a broad, self-serve-style rollout rather than a handful of bespoke engagements.

## 2. Business Evaluations & Implementation, in Depth

**Approach:** Conducted full **business evaluations for six auto dealerships** and implemented the resulting changes directly, rather than just handing over a report.

**Outcome:** The six dealerships that received a full evaluation and implementation saw their **monthly PAT (profit after tax) lift by ~6 percentage points.**

## What this demonstrates

Early evidence of a hybrid analyst/PM working style, at two different scales simultaneously — a broad, scalable rollout (trackers + training, 200+ dealerships) alongside a small number of deep, accountable engagements (business evaluations, implemented not just reported, tied to a hard outcome metric). Not just producing an analysis, but implementing the resulting change and being accountable for the business outcome — the same "own it end to end" pattern that shows up in every later role.

## One-line pitch

At Prashaste, built sales and finance trackers used across 200+ auto dealerships (with 25+ training sessions delivered), and separately led full business evaluations for six dealerships, implementing the changes directly and lifting their monthly PAT by ~6 percentage points.
