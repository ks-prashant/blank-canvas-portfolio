Why the fintech years matter for AI work

Before AI, I spent two years shipping products where being wrong cost real money — RBI compliance, KYC, lending to 6,000 retailers. That habit is why the AI I build asks permission before it acts, checks a result is genuine before trusting it, and reads an outcome rather than guessing at one.
