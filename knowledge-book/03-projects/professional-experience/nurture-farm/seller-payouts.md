# Automated Seller Payouts — nurture.farm

**Identity:** Built at nurture.farm, an MSME marketplace app under UPL. Owned end to end by Prashant Singh, Product Manager (promoted from APM during this tenure). 05/2021–11/2022.

**Status:** Shipped, live across the seller base during his tenure.

---

## 1. Problem

Seller payouts were previously manual, slow, and carried compliance risk.

## 2. Approach

Built automated payout workflows — reconciliation logic, scheduled payouts, and exception handling — across **six payment modes** (including points, cheque, payment gateway, wallets, credit, etc.), ensuring RBI compliance throughout.

## 3. Outcome

- **99% on-time payment rate.**
- Reduced manual effort and operational risk.

## 4. What this demonstrates

Regulated-payments operational discipline — automating a compliance-sensitive workflow (RBI-governed payouts) across six distinct payment modes while holding a 99% on-time rate, not just building a happy-path automation and leaving edge cases manual.

## 5. One-line pitch

Automated RBI-compliant seller payouts across six payment modes for an MSME marketplace, with reconciliation and exception handling built in — achieving a 99% on-time payment rate.
